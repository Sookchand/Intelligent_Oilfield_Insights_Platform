📊 Architecture Overview

The Challenge: Halliburton manages 10,000+ wells, rigs, and sensors globally. Traditional dashboards fail due to information overload.

The Solution: A three-tier "Global-to-Local" drill-down strategy powered by multi-agent AI:

Critical: >15% assets failing
Warning: >20% with alerts
Healthy: All systems normal
SQL Agent: Production data
Graph Agent: Dependencies
Vector Agent: HSE reports
Reasoning Agent: Synthesis
